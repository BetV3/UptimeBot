What this checks

  • Uptime from 3 US cities: an HTTP request from New York, Dallas and San Francisco, following redirects, expecting a 200.
  • Response time: the full request time from each location, so you can see where a site is slow.
  • Blocked locations: a site that answers one city and refuses another usually has a firewall or host rule blocking data-center networks.
  • SSL certificate: days until expiry and whether browsers trust it.

Limits

Up to {{ max_sites }} public sites per check and a few checks per hour per visitor. Results are kept for 24 hours at a private link, then deleted. We do not use the sites you enter for anything else.
